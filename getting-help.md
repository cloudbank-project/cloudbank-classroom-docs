# Getting Help

If you are a student, start with your instructor.

## Students, start with your instructor

Your instructor can fix most problems: signing in with a personal account instead of your school
account, using the hub address for a different course, or not being on the roster yet.

Your instructor can also open your server and see your notebooks as you left them, which is usually
faster than explaining the problem over email. See [Admin Dashboard](administration.md).

## Instructors, what to ask us for

Email us for:

- A package added to your course environment. See [requesting packages](environments/packages.md).
- Admin access on your hub, if the [admin dashboard](administration.md) doesn't load for you.
- A large dataset you want in the shared folder instead of in each student's copy. See
  [sharing data and course materials](files/sharing.md).
- [Jupyter AI](ai-tools/jupyter-ai.md) or [JupyTutor](ai-tools/jupytutor.md) on your hub.
- Access to the hosted Otter grader. See [grading with Otter](grading/otter-grader.md).
- A hub for a campus that doesn't have one yet. See [hub URL](getting-started/hub-url.md) for the
  current list.

Changes to the hub image take time, so ask before the term starts, not the week you need it.

## How to reach us

Email <help@cloudbank.org> to open a help desk ticket. Use this for anything about your hub or your
course.

The [community forum](https://community.cloudbank.org) is for general questions about working in the
cloud and for sharing what has worked in other courses.

The [ACCESS CloudBank affinity group](https://support.access-ci.org/affinity-groups/cloudbank)
posts announcements and outage notices. Join it now so you hear about outages when they happen.

For all of CloudBank's support options, including contacts for the cloud providers, see
[cloudbank.org/help](https://www.cloudbank.org/help).

## What to include

Include these and we can usually answer in one email:

- Your hub address and your username.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- The exact package name and version, if you are asking for a package.
- The student's username, if the problem is on a student's account.

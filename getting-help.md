# Getting Help

Where to go depends on what you need. If you are taking a course, that is almost always your
instructor.

## Students, start with your instructor

Most of what goes wrong for a student is something the course can settle: signing in with a personal
account instead of an institutional one, working from a hub address that belongs to a different
course, or not being on the roster yet.

Your instructor can also open your server and see your notebooks in the state you left them, which
settles most problems faster than describing them over email. [Admin Dashboard](administration.md)
covers how that works.

## Instructors, what to ask CloudBank for

These are the requests that need someone on our side:

- A package added to your course environment. See [requesting packages](environments/packages.md).
- Admin access on your hub, if the [admin dashboard](administration.md) does not load for you.
- A dataset large enough to belong in the shared folder rather than in each student's copy. See
  [sharing data and course materials](files/sharing.md).
- [Jupyter AI](ai-tools/jupyter-ai.md) or [JupyTutor](ai-tools/jupytutor.md) set up on your hub.
- A hub for a campus that does not have one yet. See [hub URL](getting-started/hub-url.md) for the
  current list.

Anything that changes the hub image takes lead time, so ask before the term rather than the week you
need it.

## How to reach CloudBank

Email <help@cloudbank.org> to open a help desk ticket, and your issue is escalated as needed. This
is the right place for anything specific to your hub or your course.

The [community forum](https://community.cloudbank.org) is for general questions about working in the
cloud and for comparing notes on what has worked for other courses.

The [ACCESS CloudBank affinity group](https://support.access-ci.org/affinity-groups/cloudbank)
carries announcements and outage notices. Join it before you need it, rather than while wondering
whether a hub is down.

For the full list of CloudBank support options, including contacts for the cloud providers
themselves, see [cloudbank.org/help](https://www.cloudbank.org/help).

## What to include

A request that arrives with these gets answered in one round instead of three:

- Your hub address and your username.
- What you did, and what happened instead of what you expected.
- The exact package name and version, for a package request.
- The student's username, when you are reporting a problem on someone else's behalf.

# ACCESS Allocations

CloudBank Classroom hubs are funded through [ACCESS](https://access-ci.org/), the NSF-supported
programme that allocates computing to research and teaching. Each institution using a hub needs its
own ACCESS project, and the institution's own instructor has to request it — it cannot be done on
your behalf.

This page is for instructors. Students do not need an ACCESS account to use a hub.

:::{note}
There is no cost, and no NSF award is required.
:::

## Why it matters

Usage on your hub is reported against your institution's ACCESS project. Without one, your students'
work is not counted, which is what the programme uses to justify continued funding for hubs like
yours. A hub keeps working either way — this is reporting, not access control — but an
unregistered institution is invisible in the numbers.

## Requesting a project

The tier you want is **Explore ACCESS**, the entry level. You can move up later if a course
outgrows it.

1. **Create an ACCESS account** at [allocations.access-ci.org](https://allocations.access-ci.org/),
   using your institutional email address. An existing XSEDE account carries over.
2. **Make yourself the project lead.** For a classroom allocation this is the instructor teaching
   the course.
3. **Prepare the request.** You need a project title and an overview describing your goals, how you
   expect to use the resources, and any software you need. You also attach two PDFs: **your CV or
   resume**, and a course syllabus for classroom work. Grant
   details are optional.

   :::{note}
   A **résumé is fine** — this does not have to be a full academic CV. Three pages is the maximum
   allowed, not a target, and a short one is perfectly acceptable. This step stalls more requests
   than it should, usually because people set it aside to write something longer.
   :::
4. **Submit it.** From the opportunities page choose Explore ACCESS, complete the form, tick the
   ACCESS Credits box, and submit. You will get an email confirming receipt.
5. **Send the project number to CloudBank.** It looks like `CIS261543`. Nothing connects your hub
   to your allocation until someone at CloudBank records it.

:::{warning}
The project overview is read by a human and is the most common reason a request stalls. Write at
least **three distinct sentences** — what the course is, what students will do on the hub, and what
software it uses. One sentence, or three restatements of the same sentence, tends to come back.
:::

A decision on an Explore request usually arrives by the next business day.

## If it does not go through

Three failures account for nearly all of the ones we have seen, and none is anything you did wrong.

**Your institution's email domain is not recognised.** The registration rejects the address
outright. The domain has to be added on the ACCESS side, which needs a support ticket rather than
another attempt — tell CloudBank and it can be raised for you.

**The verification email never arrives.** Check spam first, but if it genuinely does not come,
retrying usually does not help either. This one also needs a ticket.

**The account exists but no request was ever submitted.** This is the most common of the three and
the easiest to miss: creating the ACCESS account is a separate step from requesting the allocation.
If you are not sure which you did, sign in and look for the project under **My Projects**. No
project means step 4 above has not happened yet.

:::{note}
If you are stuck on any of these, say so rather than retrying. Two of the three need someone to
open a ticket with ACCESS, and a stalled request can sit for months otherwise.
:::

[Getting help](getting-help.md) covers where to ask.

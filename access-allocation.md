# ACCESS Allocations

CloudBank Classroom hubs are funded through [ACCESS](https://access-ci.org/), the NSF-supported
program that allocates computing to research and teaching. Each institution using a hub needs its
own ACCESS project, and the institution's own instructor has to request it — it cannot be done on
your behalf.

This page is for instructors. Students do not need an ACCESS account to use a hub.

:::{note}
There is no cost, and no NSF award is required. The whole process takes a few minutes, and nothing
changes on your hub once it is done.
:::

## Why it matters

Usage on your hub is reported against your institution's ACCESS project. Without one, your students'
work is not counted, which is what the program uses to justify continued funding for hubs like
yours. A hub keeps working either way — this is reporting, not access control — but an
unregistered institution is invisible in the numbers.

## Before you start

The steps are also published on CloudBank's own
[ACCESS CloudBank for Classroom](https://www.cloudbank.org/training/access-cloudbank-classroom)
page. Have these ready:

- **Course title(s).**
- **A course abstract.** It is published on the
  [ACCESS Impact pages](https://allocations.access-ci.org/current-projects), so write it for a
  public reader. This template works:

  > Our university, [University Name], requests access to the CloudBank Classroom resource to
  > support [Course Title] - [Fall/Winter/Spring] [Year]. This course is designed to [Course
  > abstract]. We expect approximately [Number of Students] [undergraduate/graduate] students will
  > utilize this environment for interactive assignments and data analysis, significantly enhancing
  > their practical skills.

- **Your course syllabus as a PDF.**
- **The number of students** and **the dates of the class.**

## Step 1: Create an ACCESS account and log in

1. Go to the [ACCESS user registration page](https://operations.access-ci.org/identity/new-user)
   and enter your institutional email address. It must match your institution: `gmail.com`,
   `yahoo.com` and other personal addresses are rejected. An existing XSEDE account carries over.
2. Move through the registration prompts: verify your email, fill in the required registration
   information, and accept the acceptable use policy. Be sure to fill in **Country of residence**,
   **Citizenship** and **Academic status**.
3. Follow the [instructions to log in and set up your new account](https://operations.access-ci.org/identity/new-user-setup).

## Step 2: Request an Explore ACCESS project

1. Log in to the [ACCESS allocations site](https://allocations.access-ci.org/login). For a
   classroom allocation, the project lead is the instructor teaching the course.
2. Click **Request New Project** in the top right and choose **Request an Explore ACCESS project**.
   Explore is the entry tier; you can move up later if a course outgrows it.

   :::{figure} images/access-request-new-project.png
   :alt: The ACCESS My Projects page with the Request New Project menu open, listing Explore, Discover, Accelerate and Maximize ACCESS projects.

   Choose the first option, **Request an Explore ACCESS project**.
   :::

3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title:** the title of your class, or the type of courses if there are several.
   - **Public overview:** your course abstract from [Before you start](#before-you-start).
   - **Keywords:** related to your course(s).
   - **How do you plan to use this project:** select **Classroom / Training**.
   - **Fields of Science:** related to your course(s), for example *Science and Engineering
     Education*.
   - **Documents:** choose **Syllabus** as the type and upload your PDF. If the form also asks for
     a CV, **a résumé is fine** — it does not have to be a full academic CV, and three pages is the
     maximum allowed, not a target.
   - **Available Resources:** tick **CloudBank Classroom**, then fill in your institution name,
     the estimated number of students, and the dates of your classes (tick more than one if
     needed).
4. Click **Submit**. You will see a summary page and get an email confirming receipt.

:::{figure} images/access-step2-cloudbank.png
:alt: Step 2 on the CloudBank ACCESS page, listing the form fields: title, public overview, keywords, how you plan to use the project, fields of science, documents, and available resources.

Step 2 as it appears on the CloudBank page, with a note for every field.
:::

:::{warning}
The public overview is read by a human and is the most common reason a request stalls. Write at
least **three distinct sentences** — what the course is, what students will do on the hub, and what
software it uses. One sentence, or three restatements of the same sentence, tends to come back.
:::

## Step 3: Send your request number to CloudBank

The summary page after you submit shows a **Request Number**, which looks like `CIS261543`. Email
it to your CloudBank contact. CloudBank reviews and approves the request and will contact you if
anything else is needed. Nothing connects your hub to your allocation until the number is recorded,
so this step matters as much as the request itself.

## Renewing

Explore allocations last **one year**. To keep the allocation past that, submit a renewal request
before it expires — see the
[ACCESS instructions to renew a project](https://allocations.access-ci.org/how-to#renew-a-project).

## If it does not go through

Three failures account for nearly all of the ones we have seen, and none is anything you did wrong.

**Your institution's email domain is not recognized.** The registration rejects the address
outright with *Unknown Email Domain*. The domain has to be added on the ACCESS side, which needs a
support ticket rather than another attempt — tell CloudBank and it can be raised for you.

**The verification email never arrives.** Check spam first, but if it genuinely does not come,
retrying usually does not help either. This one also needs a ticket.

**The account exists but no request was ever submitted.** This is the most common of the three and
the easiest to miss: creating the ACCESS account (Step 1) is a separate step from requesting the
project (Step 2). If you are not sure which you did, log in and look for the project under **My
Projects**. No project means Step 2 has not happened yet.

:::{note}
If you are stuck on any of these, say so rather than retrying. Two of the three need someone to
open a ticket with ACCESS, and a stalled request can sit for months otherwise.
:::

[Getting help](getting-help.md) covers where to ask.

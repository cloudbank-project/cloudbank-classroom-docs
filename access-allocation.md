# ACCESS Allocations

The NSF funds CloudBank Classroom hubs through [ACCESS](https://access-ci.org/), and we are asked
to register each institution that uses a hub. The instructor at each institution needs to make the
request. We can't do it for you.

This page is for instructors. Students do not need an ACCESS account.

:::{note}
It is free and takes a few minutes. You don't need an NSF award, and nothing changes on your hub.
:::

## Why it matters

We report your hub's usage to the NSF under your institution's ACCESS project. If there is no
project, your students' work doesn't get counted, and those numbers are how we keep the hubs
funded. Your hub will keep working either way.

## Before you start

CloudBank has the same steps on its
[ACCESS CloudBank for Classroom](https://www.cloudbank.org/training/access-cloudbank-classroom)
page. Have these ready:

- **Course title(s).**
- **A course abstract.** ACCESS publishes it on the
  [ACCESS Impact pages](https://allocations.access-ci.org/current-projects). You can use this
  template:

  > Our university, [University Name], requests access to the CloudBank Classroom resource to
  > support [Course Title] - [Fall/Winter/Spring] [Year]. This course is designed to [Course
  > abstract]. We expect approximately [Number of Students] [undergraduate/graduate] students will
  > utilize this environment for interactive assignments and data analysis, significantly enhancing
  > their practical skills.

- **Your course syllabus as a PDF.**
- **The number of students** and **the dates of the class.**

There are two parts to this. First you get an ACCESS account, then you request the project. The
account is the part that trips people up, and there are two ways through it depending on your
school.

## Step 1: Get an ACCESS account

Go to the [ACCESS registration page](https://account.access-ci.org/register) and enter your school
email address. Gmail, Yahoo and other personal addresses won't work.

:::{figure} images/access-register-email.png
:alt: The ACCESS Registration page with an Email Address field and a CONTINUE button. A sidebar lists four stages: Verify Email, Required Registration Information, Acceptable Use Policy, Your ACCESS ID.

Enter your school email address and click **CONTINUE**.
:::

Enter the six-digit code. It comes from **registry@cilogon.org**, so check your spam folder if you
don't see it.

:::{figure} images/access-verify-code.png
:alt: The Verify Email Address page with six boxes for the verification code and a CONTINUE button.

The code comes from registry@cilogon.org.
:::

What happens next depends on your school.

### If your school works with CILogon

ACCESS offers to sign you in with your school login. Use it. Finish the registration form, accept
the acceptable use policy, and you are done with this step. When you log in later, pick your school
from the identity provider list.

Fill in the form with your **Institution**, **Department**, **Academic Status**, **Country of
Residence** and **Country of Citizenship**. If your school isn't in the Institution list, use the
**organization request form** link under the field, or let us know.

### If your school does not work with CILogon

Some schools aren't in CILogon at all. Others are in it but sign in through Microsoft, which does
not work here. If you aren't sure which you are, try your school login first. If it fails, come
back and do this instead.

Make an ACCESS password and log in with that. It never touches your school's login.

If you already have an ACCESS account, you'll see a red box with your ACCESS ID in it. Write the ID
down. Old XSEDE accounts still count, so you may have one without knowing.

:::{figure} images/access-existing-account.png
:alt: A red Existing Account banner reading "You already have an ACCESS account. Your ACCESS ID is smorris3. You can login or reset your password."

Your ACCESS ID is in the red box. Write it down before you click anything.
:::

:::{warning}
Don't register a second time. Your ACCESS ID is the same as your old XSEDE username, and a second
ID is a second account that we can't match to your hub.
:::

Click **reset your password**. The password needs 12 to 64 characters and three of these:
lowercase letters, uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols.

:::{figure} images/access-change-password.png
:alt: The Change ACCESS Password page with Password and Confirm Password fields and an UPDATE PASSWORD button.

Set your new password.
:::

:::{figure} images/access-password-updated.png
:alt: A green Password Updated banner above the ACCESS Account page, which has LOGIN and REGISTER buttons.

Now click **LOGIN**.
:::

At the identity provider screen, choose **ACCESS CI (XSEDE)** and use your ACCESS ID and your new
password.

:::{figure} images/access-cilogon-access-ci.png
:alt: The CILogon Select an Identity Provider screen with ACCESS CI (XSEDE) chosen in the dropdown, a Remember this selection checkbox, and a Log On button.

Choose **ACCESS CI (XSEDE)**, then click **Log On**.
:::

## Step 2: Request an Explore ACCESS project

Having an account is not the same as having a project. This is the step people skip.

1. Go to the [ACCESS allocations site](https://allocations.access-ci.org/login) and log in. The
   instructor teaching the course should be the project lead.

   :::{figure} images/access-allocations-projects.png
   :alt: The ACCESS Allocations My Projects page reading "You don't have any projects yet", with a REQUEST NEW PROJECT button in the top right.

   Click **ALLOCATIONS** in the top menu to get here.
   :::

2. Click **REQUEST NEW PROJECT** in the top right and choose **Request an Explore ACCESS project**.

   :::{figure} images/access-request-new-project.png
   :alt: The ACCESS My Projects page with the Request New Project menu open, listing Explore, Discover, Accelerate and Maximize ACCESS projects.

   Choose **Request an Explore ACCESS project**.
   :::

3. Fill in the form:

   :::{figure} images/access-explore-form.png
   :alt: The Explore ACCESS request form showing the Request Information section with Project Title and Public overview fields.

   The form opens on Project Title and Public overview.
   :::

   - **Project Title:** the title of your class, or the type of courses if you have more than one.
   - **Public overview:** your course abstract from [Before you start](#before-you-start).
   - **Keywords:** words related to your course(s).
   - **How do you plan to use this project:** select **Classroom / Training**.
   - **Fields of Science:** related to your course(s), for example *Science and Engineering
     Education*.
   - **Documents:** choose **Syllabus** as the type and upload your PDF. If it also asks for a CV,
     a short résumé is fine. Three pages is the limit.
   - **Available Resources:** check **CloudBank Classroom**, then fill in your institution name,
     the estimated number of students, and the dates of your classes.
4. Click **Submit**. You will see a summary page and get a confirmation email.

:::{warning}
Someone at ACCESS reads the public overview, and a short one is the most common reason a request
gets sent back. Write at least three sentences: what the course is, what students will do on the
hub, and what software they will use.
:::

## Step 3: Send us your request number

The summary page shows a **Request Number** that looks like `CIS261543`. Email it to your
CloudBank contact. We will approve the request and contact you if we need anything else. Your hub
isn't connected to your allocation until we have this number.

## Renewing

Explore allocations last **one year**. Submit a renewal before it expires. See the
[ACCESS instructions to renew a project](https://allocations.access-ci.org/how-to#renew-a-project).

## If it doesn't go through

These are the problems we see most often.

**Your school isn't recognized.** You get an *Unknown Email Domain* error, or your school isn't in
the Institution list. ACCESS has to add it, which usually takes 1 to 2 days. Use the
**organization request form** link under the Institution field, or let us know and we will open a
ticket.

**The verification email never shows up.** It comes from **registry@cilogon.org**. Check your spam
folder. Some schools block this address, so if it isn't there, ask your IT department to allow it,
or let us know. This may also need a ticket.

**Your school login doesn't work.** Go back to
[If your school does not work with CILogon](#if-your-school-does-not-work-with-cilogon) and make an
ACCESS password instead.

**You forgot your ACCESS ID.** Put your email into the
[registration page](https://account.access-ci.org/register) again. It shows you the ID you already
have. Reset your password at
[Change ACCESS Password](https://account.access-ci.org/password) if you need to.

**You created the account but never submitted the request.** This is the most common one. Step 1
and Step 2 are separate. Log in and look under **My Projects**. If there is no project there, do
Step 2.

:::{note}
If you get stuck, email us instead of trying again. Most of these need a ticket with ACCESS, and a
stuck request can sit for months.
:::
